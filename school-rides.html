How it works

Set up once, ride all year

Everything — setup, pricing, tracking, and billing — happens in the SkoolMotion parent app.

1

Add your child and school

Create your child's profile in the app and pick their school from our directory of Massachusetts schools.

2

Set your pickup address and schedule

Choose morning pickup, afternoon return, or both, and the days your child rides.

3

See your exact price before you commit

Your fare is distance-based — computed from your home to the school — and shown in full in the app before you subscribe. No surprises.

4

Meet your driver, track every ride

A certified professional driver is assigned to your route. Every run has live GPS tracking, arrival notifications, and a verification PIN at pickup.

Pricing

Distance-based, shown up front

You pay for your route, not a flat national rate. The app quotes your exact price from your home address before you subscribe.

From $15

Per one-way ride

Fares are computed from the distance between your home and your child's school. Short hops cost less; longer routes cost more. Morning-only, afternoon-only, or round-trip — you choose.

15% off

Sibling discounts

Your second child rides at 15% off, and discounts grow up to 30% for additional siblings on the same run — current rates, applied automatically in the app.

Included

The professional layer

Certified drivers, certified and inspected vehicles, backup drivers when yours is out, live GPS on every run, PIN-verified pickup, and support. This is why subscriptions cost more than a neighborhood carpool.

Plans

Three ways to pay

Same rides, same drivers, same service — pick the billing that fits your family's planning.

Monthly

Maximum flexibility

Billed month to month for the school days in each month. Turn on auto-pay and never think about it again.

  • Renews monthly — adjust as your schedule changes
  • Optional auto-pay
  • Sibling discounts apply

Multi-Month

Cover a stretch at once

Pay for up to 4 months upfront in one payment — a simple way to cover a semester-length stretch of the year.

  • One payment, up to 4 months
  • No monthly billing to manage in between
  • Sibling discounts apply
