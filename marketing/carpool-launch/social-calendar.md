# Carpool Launch — Social Calendar (Fri Jul 24 → Fri Jul 31, 2026)

> **HARD GATE (decision #10):** NO reimbursement or cost-share dollar amounts, and NO per-trip savings claims, in any post — anywhere. Membership prices ($89/mo, $79/mo founding, 10% school-year prepay) and "costs covered" / "reimbursement" language ARE allowed.

## How to use this calendar

- **Channels** (same stack as `02-CONTENT-CALENDAR.md` / `06-TOOL-SETUP-GUIDE.md`): Instagram (feed + Stories + Reels), Facebook Page, Facebook parent groups, TikTok, X, Nextdoor, and email (Beehiiv). Schedule via Publer at the standing times: IG 10:00 AM, FB 12:00 PM, TikTok 6:00 PM, X 8:00 AM / 12:00 PM / 5:00 PM.
- **Four rotating themes** (plan §9.4): ① founding-driver countdown ("100 spots"), ② founding-family countdown ("200 spots — $79/mo locked, pay nothing until your route goes live"), ③ trust pillars (state-certified drivers, registry-screened, live-tracked), ④ neighborhood-ambassador referral asks.
- **Copy rules (§9.2 — non-negotiable):** warm words in headlines; the benchmark line ("cleared and certified to the same standard Massachusetts requires of school bus drivers") in subheadlines; credential names (7D, CORI, SORI) only in fine print / captions' last line. Never describe screening with "checked"-style language — always "fully vetted" / "cleared through the state's registries". Never bare "7D" in a headline or callout — say "state-certified to drive schoolchildren". Spell out "sex-offender registry". Driver money is always "reimbursement" / "costs covered" — never earnings, income, or pay, and never "volunteers". Carpool is "available in participating neighborhoods — check yours" — every family-facing post points at the availability checker.
- **Counters:** `{{driver_spots_left}}` and `{{family_spots_left}}` come from actual pre-registration counts. Only post real numbers.
