# Carpool Launch — Creative Briefs (formats used by `social-calendar.md`)

> **HARD GATE (decision #10):** NO reimbursement or cost-share dollar amounts, and NO per-trip savings claims, in any asset. Membership prices ($89/mo, $79/mo founding, 10% school-year prepay) and "costs covered" / "reimbursement" language ARE allowed.

**Mandatory copy rules on every asset (§9.2):**
1. Never describe screening with "checked"-style language — always "fully vetted" / "cleared through the state's registries".
2. Never bare "7D" in a headline or callout — plain English: "state-certified to drive schoolchildren". Credential names (Massachusetts 7D, CORI, SORI) may appear only in the smallest text layer (fine print).
3. Layering: warm headline → benchmark subheadline ("cleared and certified to the same standard Massachusetts requires of school bus drivers") → credentials by name in fine print.
4. Always spell out "sex-offender registry" (never SORI alone).
5. Driver money = "reimbursement" / "costs covered". Never earnings, income, pay, or "volunteer".
6. Family-facing assets say "available in participating neighborhoods — check yours" and point at the availability checker; never imply universal availability.

**Brand:** Purple #281C82, Pink #D31C82, Cream #FDFCFA, Outfit typeface — same system as the website and existing Canva templates. Logo: `logo.png` (light backgrounds) / `logo-white.png` (dark).

## Brief A — Founding countdown graphic (two variants)

- **Format:** Static graphic, 1080×1080 (feed) + 1080×1920 (Stories). Built in Canva with an editable counter number so it can be updated daily.
- **Message:** Scarce, honest, founding-class framing. Driver variant: "100 founding driver spots — free training, your costs covered." Family variant: "200 founding family spots — $79/mo locked for year one, $0 until your route goes live."
- **Audience:** Driver variant: Greater Boston parents who already drive a school run. Family variant: parents currently juggling the school run who want out.
- **Visual direction:** Big counter numeral in pink on deep purple (Stories) or purple on cream (feed); progress ring or seat-grid motif (100 or 200 seats, filled seats highlighted as counts climb). One small warm photo chip (parent + kids at car door). Keep it calm and premium — no alarm-red urgency clichés.
- **Copy blocks:** Headline (warm, per variant) → one-line subhead (driver: "All training paid for by SkoolMotion. Your car, your run, your costs covered." / family: "Pay nothing until a carpool route goes live near you. Available in participating neighborhoods — check yours.") → fine print (canonical credentials sentence, smallest size).
- **CTA + link:** Driver: "Claim a founding spot" → `carpool-driver.html?utm_campaign=founding-drivers-100`. Family: "Claim your founding rate" → `carpool-membership.html?utm_campaign=founding-families-200` (+ per-channel `utm_source`/`utm_medium` from the calendar).

## Brief B — "Your school run, shared" driver portrait

- **Format:** Photo for feed + 20–30s vertical video (Reel/TikTok). Phone-shot, authentic — matches the campaign's existing "authentic style" video direction.
- **Message:** You're already driving; the empty seat matters. Recruitment through identity ("trusted parent driver"), not employment. This is the §9.1 identity in visual form.
- **Audience:** School-run parents, especially those with an SUV/minivan and a fixed morning routine.
- **Visual direction:** Real parent (founder, early applicant, or friend — with release) at their own car in soft morning light, kid in the back seat, one visibly empty seat as the hero of the frame. Video beats: buckle own kid → glance at empty seat → neighbor kid waves and climbs in → both kids chatting → parent smiles at wheel. On-screen text: "You're already driving." → "Bring a neighbor's child along." → "Training free. Costs covered." No cash, wallets, or money imagery anywhere (rule 5 + hard gate).
- **Copy blocks:** Overlay text warm only; caption carries the §9.1 paragraph; fine-print credentials line as the caption's last line.
- **CTA + link:** "Claim one of 100 founding spots" → `carpool-driver.html?utm_campaign=founding-drivers-100`.

## Brief C — Trust-pillar card set

- **Format:** 3-card carousel (feed) + single stacked-badges version (Stories/X). Reusable evergreen set.
- **Message:** Warmth backed by the school-bus benchmark. One pillar per card: ① Fully vetted ② Certified to drive schoolchildren ③ Live-tracked on every run.
- **Audience:** Rider families weighing whether to trust a parent driver; secondary reassurance for prospective drivers ("this is a serious program").
- **Visual direction:** Clean icon + short warm headline per card (shield / graduation-cap-over-steering-wheel / map-pin route), purple on cream with pink accents — mirrors the website's trust-bar look. Final card carries the benchmark subheadline large and the canonical sentence ("Every SkoolMotion driver completes Massachusetts 7D certification — the state's special license for driving schoolchildren — plus state criminal and sex-offender registry screening (CORI and SORI), the same clearance required of school bus drivers.") as the fine-print layer.
- **Copy rules note:** Card headlines must never contain 7D, CORI, or SORI — those live only on the final card's fine print.
- **CTA + link:** "See how we vet every driver" → `carpool-membership.html?utm_campaign=carpool-trust` (link to the page's Trust & Safety section).

## Brief D — Availability-checker demo

- **Format:** 15–25s screen-recording vertical video (Reel/TikTok/Stories), captioned for sound-off.
- **Message:** Honesty as the hook — carpool is neighborhood-by-neighborhood and we show you the truth: routes live / drivers in certification / not yet (join the waitlist and we'll build it there). This is §9.2 rule 6 turned into content.
- **Audience:** Families who saw a countdown post and want to know "but is it near ME?"
- **Visual direction:** Real capture of carpool-membership.html on a phone: type a school name + ZIP → result card animates in. Show at least two different outcomes (e.g., "forming" then "none") so the honesty lands. End card: founding-rate lockup ($79/mo, $0 until live).
- **Copy blocks:** Opening overlay: "Is a carpool forming near you?" Closing overlay: "Check yours in 10 seconds." No availability promises beyond what the checker returns.
- **CTA + link:** "Check your neighborhood" → `carpool-membership.html?utm_campaign=founding-families-200` (the availability section).

## Brief E — Neighborhood-ambassador referral card

- **Format:** Square graphic designed to be forwarded (feed + WhatsApp/SMS-friendly), plus a plain-text version for Facebook groups and Nextdoor.
- **Message:** "Know the parent who already drives this run? Send them this." Every referred driver opens a route for the whole neighborhood — referral as neighborhood self-interest, feeding the 100-driver goal.
- **Audience:** Engaged followers and community-group members — especially parents who want carpool at their school but won't drive themselves (§14.3 exit 3).
- **Visual direction:** Feels like a passed note or route map: hand-drawn-style line from home to school with an empty-seat marker; "This route needs one driver. You know them." Warm, personal, minimal logo presence so it shares naturally.
- **Copy blocks:** Headline (warm ask) → subhead: "Free state-certification training, paid by SkoolMotion. Their car, their run, their costs covered." → footer: "100 founding spots for the 2026/27 school year."
- **CTA + link:** "Send them here" → `carpool-driver.html?utm_campaign=carpool-ambassador`; family-side variant "Want a seat instead?" → `carpool-membership.html?utm_campaign=carpool-ambassador`.

## Brief F — Founder to-camera launch video

- **Format:** 45–60s vertical video (TikTok/Reel), casual to-camera — same format as the existing founder-story slots in `02-CONTENT-CALENDAR.md`.
- **Message:** Why parent carpool exists (there aren't enough professional drivers for every neighborhood; parents already drive these runs), what founding drivers get (free training first-pick, priority matching, founding badge), and the straight promise to families (vetted like school bus drivers, live-tracked, pay nothing until a route is live).
- **Audience:** Both funnels; the credibility anchor for the whole week.
- **Script guardrails:** Say "your costs covered" / "reimbursement" — never a dollar figure, never "earn". Say "state-certified to drive schoolchildren"; credentials by name only if shown as an on-screen fine-print band. Include the availability truth ("we're starting in participating neighborhoods — check yours") rather than a universal promise.
- **Visual direction:** Founder at a family car or outside a school (not an office), morning light, one continuous take with 2–3 cutaways (empty seat, app tracking screen). End card: both counters + both links.
- **CTA + link:** "Founding spots: drivers → carpool-driver.html · families → carpool-membership.html" with `utm_campaign=founding-drivers-100` / `founding-families-200` per link.
