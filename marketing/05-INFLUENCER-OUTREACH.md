## MICRO-INFLUENCER OUTREACH (Instagram/TikTok DMs)

### How to Find Local Parent Influencers

Search these hashtags on Instagram and TikTok:
- #bostonmom, #bostonmoms, #bostondad
- #brooklinemom, #newtonmom, #cambridgemom
- #bostonparent, #bostonfamily, #bostonfamilies
- #mamalife + Boston location tag
- #bostonkids, #bostonfamilyfun

**Target profile:**
- 1,000 - 15,000 followers (micro-influencers have highest engagement)
- Posts about parenting, family life, local activities
- Located in Greater Boston
- Engaged comments (not bots)
- Ideally has school-age children

**Build a list of 20-30 accounts. Reach out to all of them.**

## INFLUENCER COMPENSATION GUIDE

| Follower Range | Offer |
|---------------|-------|
| 1K - 5K | Free month of service + referral link with earnings |
| 5K - 10K | Free month + $100-150 flat fee for 1 post + 2 stories |
| 10K - 15K | Free month + $150-250 flat fee for 1 post + 3 stories + 1 Reel |
| 15K+ | Negotiate — likely $300-500+ per post |

**Total influencer budget:** $300-500 for 3-5 micro-influencers

**What to ask for:**
- 1 feed post (showing/explaining SkoolMotion)
- 2-3 stories with link sticker to waitlist
- Honest framing: "I'm trying this new service" not "AD: USE MY CODE"
- Tag @skoolmotion in all content
