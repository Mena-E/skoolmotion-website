# SkoolMotion 30-Day Pre-Launch Marketing Plan
## Goal: Maximum Waitlist Signups Before Launch

**Budget:** $3,000+ | **Team:** Founder + Marketing Assistant (2-4 hrs/day)
**Launch Window:** ~30 days from 2026-03-31
**Service Area:** Greater Boston (Boston, Brookline, Cambridge, Newton, Somerville + surrounding)

---

## Tool Stack (Monthly Cost: ~$120-160)

| Tool | Purpose | Cost/mo | Priority |
|------|---------|---------|----------|
| **ChatGPT Plus** | All content generation | $20 | Day 1 |
| **Canva Pro** | Visual content & ad creative | $13 | Day 1 |
| **Publer** | Social media scheduling + recycling | $12 | Day 1 |
| **Beehiiv** | Email marketing + built-in referral | $0 (free) | Day 1 |
| **ManyChat** | IG/FB DM automation | $15 | Day 4 |
| **Viral Loops** | Tiered referral waitlist | $49 | Day 2 |
| **Chatbase** | Website AI chatbot | $19 | Day 5 |
| **Google Analytics 4** | Web analytics | $0 | Day 1 |
| **Microsoft Clarity** | Heatmaps & session recording | $0 | Day 1 |

## Ad Spend Allocation ($2,400-2,800/mo)

| Channel | Daily Budget | Monthly | Purpose |
|---------|-------------|---------|---------|
| **Meta Ads (FB/IG)** | $50-60/day | $1,500-1,800 | Lead gen targeting Boston parents |
| **Google Performance Max** | $25-30/day | $750-900 | Capture search intent |
| **Micro-influencers** | — | $300-500 | 3-5 local parent influencer partnerships |

**Expected Results:** At $2-5/lead, paid ads alone should generate **500-1,200 signups**. Combined with organic, referral, and community channels: **1,500-3,000+ total signups**.

---

## 30-Day Execution Timeline

### Phase 1: Foundation (Days 1-3)
- [ ] Create Instagram Business, Facebook Page, TikTok, X accounts
- [ ] Set up Publer and connect all social accounts
- [ ] Install Google Analytics 4 + Microsoft Clarity on site
- [ ] Set up Beehiiv account and import existing waitlist from Supabase
- [ ] Set up Viral Loops tiered referral program
- [ ] Generate 30 days of social content using ChatGPT (see `02-CONTENT-CALENDAR.md`)
- [ ] Design 15-20 post templates in Canva (see `06-CANVA-TEMPLATES.md`)
- [ ] Schedule first 2 weeks of posts in Publer

### Phase 2: Email & Automation (Days 3-6)
- [ ] Build 7-email nurture sequence in Beehiiv (see `01-EMAIL-SEQUENCES.md`)
- [ ] Set up ManyChat for Instagram/Facebook DM auto-replies
- [ ] Install Chatbase AI chatbot on website
- [ ] Create referral landing page with Viral Loops embed
- [ ] Test full funnel: Ad click -> Landing page -> Signup -> Email -> Referral

### Phase 3: Paid Ads Launch (Days 5-10)
- [ ] Create 6-8 Meta ad variations (see `03-AD-COPY.md`)
- [ ] Design ad creative in Canva (video + static)
- [ ] Launch Meta Lead Gen campaign targeting Boston-area parents
- [ ] Launch Google Performance Max campaign
- [ ] Set up conversion tracking (waitlist signup = conversion event)
- [ ] Monitor daily for first 3 days, then every 2-3 days

### Phase 4: Social & Community Blitz (Days 5-21)
- [ ] Post daily on Instagram, Facebook, TikTok (auto-scheduled via Publer)
- [ ] Create 3-5 Reels/TikToks per week (phone-shot, authentic style)
- [ ] Join 20+ Boston parent Facebook groups (3/day to avoid flags)
- [ ] Create Nextdoor Business Page, post weekly
- [ ] Reach out to 20 local parent micro-influencers (see `05-INFLUENCER-OUTREACH.md`)
- [ ] Contact 10 after-school programs, 5 PTAs, 5 pediatric offices

### Phase 5: Optimize & Scale (Days 14-21)
- [ ] Review analytics: which channels drive most signups?
- [ ] Kill underperforming ads, double budget on winners
- [ ] A/B test email subject lines
- [ ] Push referral program in emails and social posts
- [ ] Publish 3 blog posts for SEO + social sharing (see `04-BLOG-POSTS.md`)

### Phase 6: Final Push (Days 21-30)
- [ ] Urgency-based emails ("We launch in 10 days!")
- [ ] Waitlist milestone social posts ("1,000 families and counting!")
- [ ] Retargeting ads to website visitors who didn't sign up
- [ ] Final referral push ("Last chance for Founding Family status")
- [ ] Prep launch announcement email

---

## Daily Task Checklist (For Marketing Assistant)

### Every Day (30-45 min)
- [ ] Check and respond to Instagram/Facebook DMs and comments
- [ ] Review ManyChat conversations for any that need human touch
- [ ] Check ad performance (Meta + Google dashboards)
- [ ] Post in 2-3 Facebook parent groups (use pre-written content)

### Every 2-3 Days (30 min)
- [ ] Create 1-2 short-form videos (Reels/TikToks)
- [ ] Review Clarity heatmaps for landing page optimization
- [ ] Update waitlist count for social proof posts
- [ ] Respond to any Chatbase escalations

### Weekly (1 hour)
- [ ] Review analytics dashboard and note top performers
- [ ] Adjust ad budgets (increase winners, pause losers)
- [ ] Schedule next week's social content in Publer
- [ ] Send weekly referral reminder email via Beehiiv
- [ ] Post on Nextdoor
