## META ADS — Facebook & Instagram

### Campaign Structure
- **Objective:** Lead Generation (waitlist signups)
- **Audience:** Boston metro area (30-mile radius), ages 28-50, parents with children ages 5-17
- **Detailed targeting:** Interests in school activities, after-school programs, summer camps, parenting, PTA, education
- **Placements:** Advantage+ (let Meta optimize across Feed, Stories, Reels)
- **Budget:** $50-60/day, Advantage+ campaign budget
- **Optimization:** Conversions (waitlist signup event)
