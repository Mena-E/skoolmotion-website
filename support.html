Support & FAQ

Find answers to common questions or get in touch with our support team.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I sign up for SkoolMotion?

Download the SkoolMotion app from the App Store or Google Play. Create an account using your phone number, add your child's information and school, and choose a ride plan that works for your family. Setup takes less than 5 minutes.

How are drivers vetted?

Every SkoolMotion driver completes Massachusetts 7D certification — the state's special license for driving schoolchildren — plus state criminal and sex-offender registry screening (CORI and SORI), the same clearance required of school bus drivers. All drivers are also medically cleared by a licensed physician.

What areas do you serve?

We currently serve the Greater Boston area, including Boston, Brookline, Cambridge, Somerville, Newton, and surrounding communities. Enter your address in the app to check availability and see pricing for your specific route.

How does real-time tracking work?

Once your child's ride begins, you can track the vehicle in real-time on a map in the app. You'll also receive notifications when the driver is on the way, when your child is picked up, and when they arrive at school safely.

What's the difference between scheduled rides and on-demand?

Scheduled rides are recurring daily rides (morning, afternoon, or both) at set times—perfect for regular school transportation. On-demand rides are one-time rides you can book as needed, great for after-school activities, early dismissals, or backup when your schedule changes.

How do I cancel a ride?

You can cancel scheduled rides in the app at least 2 hours before pickup time at no charge. For on-demand rides, cancel within 5 minutes of booking for a full refund. Go to "My Rides" in the app and tap the ride you want to cancel.

Who can pick up my child?

Only individuals you've authorized in the app can receive your child at dropoff. You can add authorized pickup persons (with their photo) in the app under your child's profile. Drivers verify identity before releasing children.

What if my child has special needs?

We accommodate many special needs including car seats, booster seats, and specific pickup/dropoff instructions. Add any special requirements to your child's profile, and we'll match you with a driver who can accommodate them. Contact us if you have specific questions about accommodations.

How do payments work?

Add a credit card or debit card to your account. Scheduled ride plans are billed monthly in advance. On-demand rides are charged when you book. You can view all charges and receipts in the app under "Payment History."

What happens if the driver is late?

We monitor all rides in real-time. If a driver is running more than a few minutes late due to traffic or other issues, you'll receive a notification with an updated ETA. If a driver cannot complete the ride, we'll work to arrange a replacement or notify you immediately.

How do I update my child's school or pickup address?

Go to "My Children" in the app, tap on your child's profile, and edit the school or pickup address. Changes to scheduled rides will take effect on the next school day. Pricing may change if the distance changes significantly.

How do I delete my account?

You can request account deletion by emailing privacy@skoolmotion.com or through the app settings under "Account" → "Delete Account." Please note that any active ride plans must be canceled first, and certain records may be retained as required by law.
