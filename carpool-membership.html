Carpool Membership

$89/mo

One flat membership rate, plus your route's shared trip cost. Example — for a 5-mile roundtrip route: at the $0.70/mile IRS rate over 20 school days, the whole route's trip cost is $70 — split across 3 riding families, about $23 each. On that example route a month comes to about $112 all-in. Pre-register now, pay nothing until a route goes live near you.

Your membership is separate from the trip cost-share — the gas-and-wear reimbursement that passes 100% to your driver, itemized on every invoice. The cost-share is computed at the IRS standard mileage rate ($0.70/mile) on your route's actual miles and split across the riding families, so more neighbors on the route means a smaller share for everyone. You'll see your route's exact estimated cost in the app before you ever enroll, and billing only starts when you join a live route.

Trust & Safety

Fully vetted, every single driver

Cleared and certified to the same standard Massachusetts requires of school bus drivers.

Every SkoolMotion driver completes Massachusetts 7D certification — the state's special license for driving schoolchildren — plus state criminal and sex-offender registry screening (CORI and SORI), the same clearance required of school bus drivers.

On every carpool run you also get live GPS tracking, verification PIN pickup, and a driver whose own child is often in the car — because they're a parent at your school, driving the run they already make.
