Why partner

Built to keep your vehicles earning

School-run demand is steady, recurring, and local. We handle the families, payments, and dispatch — you run your vehicles.

Who can join

Fleets of fifty. Fleets of one.

If you operate vehicles that carry schoolchildren in Massachusetts, there's a place for you here.

Fleet operators

Van and bus companies serving schools today. Register your company, add your vehicles and drivers, and put idle capacity to work on family-booked rides.

Company details, vehicle documents, and driver credentials are reviewed by our team before going live.

Independent drivers

Certified to drive schoolchildren and running your own vehicle? Register as an independent operator — a fleet of one is welcome. Same portal, same 70% share, same weekly payouts.

You'll need your Massachusetts 7D driver certificate and vehicle documentation; every operator is fully vetted before their first ride.
